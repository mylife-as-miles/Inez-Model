# Independent identity review — full-body front v03

Candidate: `references/generated/05_body_front_v03.png`. Independently viewed against v02 and both originals, plus the artist's boot comparison sheet. A separate native-pixel comparison of denim and boots is saved as `05_v02_v03_denim_boots_comparison.png`. Review date: 2026-10-08.

## Decision and candidate choice

**Facial/front-silhouette gate passes provisionally; keep v02 as the slightly better OVERALL body reference.** v03 improves boot welt darkness but creates a small unintended denim-material regression. v03 is useful as a corrected boot-tone supplement. Face remains **33/35**; scoped score is **84.25/91 (92.6%)** versus v02's 85/91. Nine points remain reserved; neither score proves whole-turnaround acceptance.

The face, arm separation, body outline, necklace, cropped sweater and three torso stripes remain stable. There is no critical anatomy failure or visible unauthorized change in garment design. However, v03's jeans acquire stronger looping/cracked scratch-like fine texture across thighs/knees than v02 or B. This pattern is not evidence of Inez's denim construction and should not be baked into model textures.

The boot edge no longer has a conspicuous gold appearance. A faint warm-dark stitch tone remains at close inspection; matching a specific thread color is not established by B's limited-resolution boots. The dark overall effect is improved. This local change is smaller in visual impact than the new denim microtexture in the full body.

## Recommended action

Do not spend another whole-body generation solely to chase these microtexture/color residuals. Preserve both versions, retain v02 for overall body/denim appearance and v03 as a dark boot-trim correction reference, and keep original B as the final material authority. Upcoming sweater/boot detail generations and actual material authoring should suppress invented denim scratch pattern and choose subdued black/charcoal boot welt/thread.

If the artist selects v03 as the public front-reference file after combining critic findings, explicitly record its denim-pattern limitation; it remains a credible front silhouette and facial identity reference.
