## Review protocol

Per candidate: inspect original A and B directly alongside candidate; record angle, crop and lighting differences; score visible categories using the user's weights (35 face, 15 body, 15 hair, 10 costume, 10 material, 10 cross-view, 5 technical); explain every deducted point. Reserve unsupported categories explicitly instead of silently giving full credit. A portrait-only or detail-only scoped score does not certify a whole-character total.

Canonical acceptance needs total ≥90/100 and facial identity ≥32/35, no critical anatomy error and no unauthorized redesign. A generated true profile can be internally plausible but is always a proposed reconstruction of unseen original anatomy; it cannot certify historical profile accuracy. Cross-view scores require actual cross-view comparison and remain provisional while views are missing.

Highest-priority failure checks: narrower nose, rounder/larger eyes, shorter lower face, sharply pointed chin, over-slim torso, sleek/stylized hair, missing sweater stripes, incorrect pendant scale, smooth beauty-filter skin.
