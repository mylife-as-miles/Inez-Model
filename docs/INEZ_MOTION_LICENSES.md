# Motion and model licences for Inez's animation

Every motion that reaches Inez carries its source and licence in its intermediate-skeleton metadata (`meta.source`, `meta.licence`), in its retarget report (`animation/qa/*_retarget.json`), and in the viewer's clip label. A clip whose source terms do not permit commercial inclusion must not ship in THREE BEDROOM.

## Sources in use

| Clip | Source | Terms | Ships? |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Idle`, `Walk`, `Run`, `LookAround`, `TurnLeft/Right`, `CrouchDown/Up`, `Crouch`, `Blink`, `Expr_*` | Procedural, generated by `tools/inez/animation_build.py` and `glb_expression_clips.py` on the CC0 MakeHuman rig | Project-owned | Yes |

**Requested acknowledgement:** "The data used in this project was obtained from mocap.cs.cmu.edu. The database was created with funding from NSF EIA-0196217."

The raw ASF/AMC files are kept in the repository as provenance. They must not be distributed as a standalone motion pack.

## Rules

1. Record the source and licence in the intermediate skeleton before retargeting. The adapters do this.
2. Never relabel a clip's source. The viewer shows `procedural`, `CMU mocap`, `TERRA` or `synthetic test` from the clip manifest.
3. Research-only inputs (AMASS, SMPL-H, SMPL-X) need a commercial licence and legal sign-off before any derived clip ships.
4. Keep the acknowledgements in the game credits for every external source that asks for one.
